01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Safety isn't a final step.
It's part of the flow.

Sentinel places two rule-based checkpoints around a locally running LLM. The first examines the incoming request. The second reviews the model's generated response before it is shown to the user.

The goal is to make the decision path visible: what was checked, which stage ran, and whether the response was allowed or replaced by a safe fallback.

02 / WHY GUARDRAILS MATTER

Powerful models need
clear boundaries.

Cloud environments handle valuable workloads and sensitive information. When AI assistants are used to explain or support cloud operations, unsafe requests and unsafe generated content need to be considered at more than one point.

06 / SCOPE & NEXT STEPS

Make the safety
path visible.

Sentinel is an educational prototype exploring a simple principle: AI applications in security-sensitive contexts should make their checks explicit, understandable and testable.

Future work could include stronger policy engines, structured audit logs, role-aware access controls, adversarial testing and integration with cloud-native security tooling.
